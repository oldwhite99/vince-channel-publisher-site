Vince Channel Publisher

Vince Channel Publisher is a private desktop automation tool used by the channel owner to upload and manage videos on their own YouTube channels.

Purpose

The application uses Google OAuth authorization to verify the selected YouTube channel, upload videos, and check upload and processing status.

The application is currently intended for use only by its owner. It is not offered as a public service and does not provide access to third-party YouTube accounts.
