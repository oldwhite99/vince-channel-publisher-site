Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Vince Channel Publisher accesses, uses, stores, and protects Google and YouTube account information.

Information accessed

The application may access the authenticated user's YouTube channel identifier and video information required to verify the selected channel, upload videos, and check upload and processing status.

How information is used

Google user data is used only to perform actions requested by the authenticated channel owner. The application does not use Google user data for advertising, profiling, or unrelated purposes.

Storage and security

OAuth credentials are stored locally on the user's Windows computer using Windows Credential Manager. Publishing task records are stored in a local SQLite database.

Sharing of information

The application does not sell, rent, share, or transfer Google user data to third parties.

Data retention and deletion

Local credentials and task records are retained until the user removes them. The user may revoke the application's access at any time through their Google Account security settings.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy , including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact laobaiplaycoin@gmail.com .
